      How it works

      1. Encrypted audio, public. Every 4-second piece of a track is locked with its own key. Anyone can download the locked audio; CashCast sells only the keys.
      2. One approval. Your wallet funds a tiny Bitcoin Cash contract with your budget. That is the only thing you sign.
      3. Pay as you listen. Your browser signs a running total with a throwaway key — no transaction per second, no fees per second.
      4. Settle once. When you stop, CashCast takes the signed total (90% to the artist, 10% to CashCast). The contract forces the rest back to your wallet.
      5. No trust needed. If CashCast vanished, anyone could send your whole budget back to you after about a day — the contract allows nothing else.

      Contract: CashCastChannel.cash (CashScript, ~240 bytes) · security notes
